# Goblin Territory and marine World Preview

This iteration is isolated to the World Preview channel (or explicit test flag). The existing three exploration island coordinates, original cave indexes, mine IDs and Alpha release channel are retained.

## Goblin Territory

A fourth, northern exploration island is centered at (0,-207), nominal radius 58. Its initial template has a surface terrace, eleven roofed structures, reserved streets, four surface guards, a three-wide descending cave entry, a grand avenue, crossroads, throne hall, barracks, forge, treasury and bone shrine, with five underground guards. Room dimensions and roles vary by seed/cycle. The terrain cave mask and section renderer use the same authoritative floor and ceiling cells. The current template is a connected single-floor underground district network, not a general multi-floor spiral or WFC city. Buildings reuse the existing architecture and cutaway code; goblins use the existing creature art and combat. No permanent quest or dungeon loot is issued by the experimental guards. The guard population is streamed rather than a persistent encounter lifecycle.

The preview world map exposes Goblin City travel and regeneration controls. Regeneration increments the local layout cycle, validates connectivity and plot overlap before publishing, and requires the player to be outside the territory. It invalidates relevant streamed geometry and cave section caches without changing other cave cells. The cycle is currently session-local; full persistent regeneration transactions and dynamic territory events are not implemented.

## Island biomes and sea front

Named biome profiles: rocky highlands/rocky shoals, temperate woodland/kelp coast, warm meadow/sandbanks, goblin badlands/cold reef. Preview inland material differentiation uses the existing atlas; the submerged coastal shelf extends up to nine cells beyond the land mask. This is a starting biome layer, not complete biome-specific forests, creatures, snow or weather. Existing dry island radii intentionally stay unchanged for save compatibility. Future larger island footprints should be introduced with versioned world generation.

## Ocean and trinket

Nearby seeded shoals are streamed on a 32m radius; eight small original voxel fish per school share a MultiMesh. Local cohesion, alignment, separation and containment are bounded. Five rarity ring colors indicate reward size. With a fishing net equipped, interact to cast, then interact during the bite window. The current reward is 1–5 existing shrimp items with an eight-second cooldown; fish inventory species, persistent school depletion and full fishing UI are not yet implemented. No external fish asset is used.

The preview Workbench adds a Wooden Rowboat recipe (30 wood, 10 fibre). It uses the trinket equipment slot and displays an automatically deployed boat visual in swimming water, increasing speed by 1.8x. Existing swimmer collision and buoyancy remain in use; real boat physics, boarding, oars, seats and boat-specific water collision are future work.

## Character inventory

Wider landscape screens show character equipment beside bag items; preview display subtracts the worn item from the bag quantity, tap a bag item then its compatible slot to equip/swap, and the existing native drag target remains available. Preview crafting no longer silently overwrites a character slot. Quick tool selection switches only equipped tools. Returning gear to bag remains an explicit action. This is a presentation and interaction correction, not a separate equipment-instance ownership/save migration. Existing saves keep shared aggregate counts; the future transactional migration must account for crafting, bag capacity, gear quality, death/tombstone, multiplayer and tool selection before physically separating slots from bag inventory. Native Android drag/swipe, detail card sizing, tool icon and layout need graphical testing.

The dedicated preview regression at a31da79 passed generation, regeneration, streamed city checks, shoal creation and rowboat equipment/deployment. Later sea shelf and explicit equipment selection changes have added assertions and require fresh verification. The earlier general settlement checks passed. Existing updater/changelog smoke issue remains a release blocker. No normal Alpha promotion. Preview APK publication is asynchronous GitHub CI; verify completed release assets before giving a download link.


## Shared wave field and rowboat presentation

The rowboat now launches with a 0.76-second visual overhead lift/hop/drop, contact splash, short damped impact bob, seated rowing animation and two oars. The previous launch regression passed. Collision and movement remain based on the existing swimmer capsule; no new rigid-body boat authority or water-driven gameplay jump.

The World Preview adapts the Roblox project's exponential swell and recursive warped-octave architecture (WaterWaveSampler.lua and OceanSurface.lua) into scripts/world/ocean_surface.gd. This is an adaptation, not a byte-for-byte or visual reproduction: six deterministic octaves, much smaller metre-scale amplitudes, no horizontal mesh displacement, and one explicit frame clock. Packed coefficient arrays are shared with the preview vertex shader. A 96m square, 96-segment near-ocean mesh follows the player on a 2m grid; the flat horizon discards its covered region and the near patch discards the existing home terrain square. The wave amplitude tapers to zero over the outer 8m. All three preview materials use the same island land mask. Normal Alpha retains its opaque renderer. The CPU wave sampler drives six hull samples, pitch and roll (bounded), wake heights, fishing ring height and fish depth; GPU mesh interpolation and damped hull response mean the rendered surface and boat pose are approximate matches, not exact physical contact. Swimming collision and underwater gameplay still use the existing flat surface.

New preview assertions check wave determinism, motion, bounded pitch/roll and flat patch seams. Headless checks cannot establish Android shader performance, alpha ordering, near/far seams or actual animation quality. The newest build and regression results are pending. Reactive displacement fields, foam intersections, storm profiles, and selectable LOD remain future work.


## Terrain feedback and separate Skeletal City

World Preview expands the western and southern dry island radii from 44/46m to 56/64m respectively, retaining their original cave horizontal route and floor samples before expansion. Settlement planning uses wider bounds, greater roughness penalties, two-cell widening, two-cell plot spacing and broader town terraces. Raised half-block road tread objects are no longer streamed or planned. Existing one-voxel auto-step movement remains; this is smoother terrain planning, not continuous ramp collision. Terrain and settlement locations can differ in existing preview saves, so use developer travel or a fresh test character if saved positions are obstructed. The first eastern mine identity is unchanged. The southern mouth has a taller side-facing roofed aperture, solid roof from the first tunnel cell and a graded exterior apron; the landing regression now samples the walkable entrance floor rather than requiring the player to climb the roof.

A fifth preview island at (186,-170), nominal radius 62m, is the independent Skeletal City. Its nine-wide, eight-high eastern mountain gateway leads into a 40-cell descent from Y=8 to Y=-4, a 22x17 central hall, a five-wide processional avenue and four seeded crypt districts. Batched original crystal and bone scenery plus cool cave lighting provide the first magical atmosphere. It uses single-interval carved cave columns, not stacked floors, a spiral or full WFC architecture. There is no persistent loot, skeleton combat population or dynamic event progression yet. Both city regeneration controls replace their own stable route index only; regeneration remains session-local and requires leaving the relevant territory. Developer travel goes to the exterior landing. Cavern view regions are bounded into small sectors and elevated surface architecture is hidden from underground views without disabling physical collision. The initial generation regression passed on 10e4bb8 (including prior game city and boat checks); later shore approach and direct skeletal streaming tests require fresh CI. Android appearance and camera performance remain unverified.


## Living villages, gatherer stockpiles and skeletal inhabitants

Goblin Territory now plans 17 roofed structures (six new infill buildings), 11 guards, two named gatherer goblins and seven interior caches. Two collection routes connect storage yards to marked supply piles on the outskirts. Workers visibly travel, perform a collection animation, and return to their storage area. A 75-second active-game work round deposits 2–5 wood, stone, fibre or occasionally coal/iron; each material is capped at 24 per worker. Loaded workers pause production when fighting or when behind their scheduled route. Unloaded workers use a bounded simulation, not physics or offline wall-clock farming. Defeating a worker stops their production for 120 active-game seconds; already gathered stock remains available. The initial supply piles are decorative and do not yet consume the player's actual mineable terrain blocks or harvest nodes. There is no procedural village building growth yet.

The fixed worker and cache IDs are seed-scoped and intentionally do not depend on a temporary layout cycle. Stock and work progress persist in the existing player save. A chest is spawned when stored stock becomes available, grants only items fitting the existing carry limit, and is removed when empty; the next deposit builds a new chest at that location. One-shot building and crypt caches use existing opened-world-object receipts. Skeletal City now has twelve existing skeleton-model guardians spread among the hall and crypts and four one-time crypt caches. These guards are streamed encounters, not a persistent kill ledger; a distinct skeletal model/AI overhaul remains outstanding.

Tests cover the updated plan counts and connectivity, pure work round and defeat policy, live gatherer spawning, partial capped pickup, destruction and renewal, saved stock and streamed skeleton population. The first integration smoke run failed the outdated 11-building expectation; the corrected test and latest route-gating revision require fresh verification. Android visual testing remains pending. Normal Alpha is unchanged.
