# Default settlement integration — Alpha first

This branch starts directly from Alpha, not City Lab. The existing game scene, Android controls, save format, starter camp and Goblin Mineshaft remain the intended baseline.

## Default content

The third exploration island (center 18,232) receives a bounded deterministic settlement. Candidate building sites avoid cave columns and their immediate neighbors; the layout preserves the original terrain height field. A weighted cardinal A* graph permits only one-block steps. It connects a hub to a reachable western shore and to up to six built-in building entrances. Buildings are selected on shallow foundations with a three-block-wide entrance. Rectangular footprints range from 5×5 to 7×7; a gravel square is planned at the public hub, and connected paths remain dirt. Built-in masonry plinths, framing, contrasting window accents, pitched roofs, parapets and chimneys vary the silhouettes. These are material accents rather than transparent windows. The default architectural kit is watchtower, smithy, storehouse, house, inn and guardhouse; generation must place at least two or omit the settlement rather than publish an inaccessible site. These are architectural buildings only, not functioning NPC shops or crafting stations yet.

After mandatory building plots and entrances are reserved, the planner attempts an additional route to a safe exterior approach near the existing third-island cave. This link is optional: it must not consume viable building plots or alter cave geometry. Road centerlines are widened onto adjacent safe cells with at most a one-block elevation change. The temporary A* graph and height tables are released after successful planning.

Road material is returned by the same island top-tile sampler used by terrain geometry and the map. No separate road overlay mesh or editor is needed. Settlement planning is lazy, only near the third island; current implementation is synchronous and should be profiled on Android. Each structure is attached to its anchor chunk and unloaded with it. This initial experiment does not yet guarantee a seamless appearance at the streaming radius.

## Camera and navigation

Buildings use a bounded voxel grammar with atlas-safe greedy rectangle merges. Floor/foundation, four wall groups, four shared corner groups and roof are independently rendered. Shared faces between visibility groups are retained so a hidden wall does not remove exposed caps from an adjacent visible group. The same physical collision bodies stay on gameplay layer 1. For an occupant at interior height, roof and camera-facing wall groups are hidden based on the actual existing camera position; the established WorldPicker building_cutaway_hidden convention skips only those bodies for selection. Neighbouring buildings whose upper bounds intersect the parallel orthographic sightlines from sampled interior floor cells also reveal their upper geometry. This conservatively hides some additional architecture to retain clear interior picking without altering physics. Leaving restores the complete exterior of every building. No global camera angle, pitch, rotation controls, cave cutaway shader or cave picking policy is replaced.

The existing click-to-move graph uses cell-center samples near the settlement and samples inside the building below the roof. Wall cells are excluded; above-floor roof destinations are rejected. Physical capsule-width checks and step limits remain authoritative.

## Verification gate

The new default_settlement regression checks repeatability, placement, roads, entrance slopes, greedy mesh reduction, eight directions of cutaway state, picker flags and restoration. The first CI attempt failed on a GDScript inference error; the explicit type correction passed the settlement unit check and the fractal archipelago workflow. A later optional-trail revision failed seed 42 because the trail reserved buildable land before plots. The revised ordering places mandatory buildings before optional routes; this revision needs a fresh successful CI run. A separate `default_settlement_game.gd` regression now loads the actual game scene with touch input mode, checks physics-backed entrance/exit path planning, eight camera orientations using the existing screen picker, and stream-out/re-entry. It is teleport-assisted and is not equivalent to a graphical device walkthrough. The widened-road and six-building geometry revision passed the three-seed terrain/mesh suite (six buildings each; 459, 414 and 402 road cells respectively). Real-game floor picking initially failed at two angles because a neighbouring roof occluded the occupied room; the new coordinated view routine addresses this and requires a fresh integration pass. Still required: full game walkthrough using touch tap and orbit, player path into/out of each building, actual pick-ray results in all orientations, stream out/re-entry, mine/save regression, Android frame time and screenshot comparison, then signed Alpha APK. The starter camp is untouched. No merge, Alpha release or APK has been made by this branch.

## Scope

The standalone PC developer tools and mod authoring are deferred. The experimental City Lab and dungeon connector solver are reference prototypes, not dependencies of this branch. Larger cities, WFC district planning, multi-island POI road networks, Skeletal City, native optimizations and physical underground connectors should follow the first validated settlement rather than replace it.

## Regional expansion

The third island's market town now shares its planner with a distinct western frontier waystation on island index 1. The western kit has a guardhouse, storehouse and house, with smaller footprints and an independent seed salt; the southern market town retains its six architectural roles. The first island and its mine are deliberately excluded from settlement planning. Each plan is cached independently on demand, with globally distinct built-in structure IDs. This is still the existing finite archipelago, not a new infinite-world coordinate implementation.

The mandatory western approach and building entrances are planned before optional routes to a cave approach and the existing eastern coastal access corridor. These endpoints are safe dry approach cells, not docks or guaranteed paths into every cave interior. A final flood-fill validates landmark reachability over the actual painted road cells with a one-block step limit before publishing the regional blueprint. Typed landmark records describe the hub, coastal approaches, cave approach and building doors. The map observes hub/coastal/cave landmarks only in its existing explored radius, saves their stable IDs as zones, and does not expose building labels yet. No NPC services or quests are added by these architectural roles.

The previous neighbour-aware cutaway revision passed the actual game picking test at all eight orientations, and the subsequent regional sampler/streamer revision passed the market-town tests. New tests exercise both profiles across three seeds, generation in reversed order, mandatory/optional route coverage, unique IDs, map discovery and state replay, and real-game waystation navigation/picking. New results must be checked separately before promotion. All changes remain on the Alpha integration PR; creator commands, new APK releases, Vulkan migration, WFC districts and underground cities are outside this iteration.

## Countryside layer

The regional blueprint now selects up to two cultivated plots for the market town and one for the waystation after road widening, from safe unreserved cells adjacent to a connected road. Each plot has a bounded rectangular footprint, at most three blocks of total natural relief with each cardinal step limited to one block, no cave overlap and a road-side entry. Fields use the existing soil tile in the authoritative surface and map samplers; no terrain heights change. Farm approach records participate in the same landmark connectivity validation and discovery system as coastal approaches.

Low crops, flowers and waystones are selected deterministically on field rows and safe roadside cells, never on a road, building footprint or protected cave column. The streamer creates at most one shared-atlas countryside MeshInstance3D per populated terrain chunk, with local mesh coordinates and no collision or per-plant nodes. These are decorative, not harvestable farms or physical signposts. Their meshes are owned by the existing chunk lifecycle, including stream-out and re-entry. Regression coverage checks deterministic placement, tile agreement, farm access, exact chunk counts, no collision children, and actual game streaming on both islands.

The previous two-island terrain and real-game navigation/camera/map tests passed. The preceding full smoke workflow also passed the fractal and Diorama targeted checks, but later failed the Android updater changelog UI checks. That failure is still a release blocker; it has not been diagnosed or changed in this world-generation iteration. The first countryside runtime check passed existing gameplay, but the new coverage test found no flat plots on the six default layouts. The planner now tries 5×7 through 3×3 plots with bounded terrace steps and no terrain flattening; this revision awaits fresh CI results. Android visual/performance testing and APK publication remain outstanding.
